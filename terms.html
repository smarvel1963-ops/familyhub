Using Family Hub

Family Hub is a family organizer: calendar, chores, lists, messages, emergency card and more. There are no Family Hub accounts. It is used by parents and other adults to organize their family. Children do not sign up; a parent adds them as a profile. By using it you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy.

Family Hub plan

Family Hub starts with a 30-day free trial of everything, for the whole family, from the day the family is made. After that it is $25 a year for the whole family, and everyone you invite uses it free. If the trial ends and nothing is bought, adding new things pauses. What you already have stays, you can still fix it, and the parent PIN, who-sees-what settings and the emergency card keep working. The plan is sold and billed through Whop. Whop's own terms apply to the purchase, renewal and cancellation. Purchases are made on Whop's website, not through Google Play. A Day Hub Pro purchase does not include Family Hub. The AI helper uses Hub AI Credits (one-time packs of 100 questions for $2.99 or 500 for $9.99, usable in any Hub app).

Your data

Your family's information lives on your phone. If your family's owner turns on sharing between phones, the shared copy is kept in the family's own space in Google Firebase, for the phones you invited. Backup to your own Google Drive is optional, and photos and document pictures are never part of it. You are responsible for keeping a backup. Phones get lost and browsers get cleared, and we cannot recover data we never had.

Family sharing and children's profiles

When your family turns on sharing, the members you invite can see what you share with them, and each person's sharing choices apply. The adults who set Family Hub up are responsible for the profiles they create for children, for who they invite, and for what is shared. The parent PIN is a kid lock for a shared phone, not a password, and it does not protect your data from someone holding your unlocked phone. Family Hub does not track anyone's location. The emergency card is something you fill in yourself: keep it up to date, and don't rely on the app alone in an emergency. Call your local emergency number. Reminders can be missed when a phone silences notifications. AI helper answers can be wrong, so check anything important.
