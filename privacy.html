Family Hub privacy policy

Family Hub is an app by Marvel Corp, part of the Hub family of apps. Last updated November 7, 2026.

The short version

What you put in Family Hub stays on your phone unless your family turns on sharing between phones (below). There are no Family Hub accounts. There are no ads, no ad trackers and no analytics in the app. Your family's information is never sold and never used for advertising. One optional AI feature sends some of it to an AI service to answer you - only when you use it - and it is listed below.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type: your family's name, each member's first name, the face and color you pick for them, their role (owner, adult, child, guest) and the birthday you enter; calendar events (what, when, who, where, notes, repeats); chores, who did them, the OKs and the stars and allowance amounts; pay-outs you log; shopping lists; the family to-do board; the emergency card (contacts and phone numbers, meeting place, plan, notes for a sitter, family doctor); the parent PIN; Grandparent Mode and other settings; family messages, check-ins, announcements and reactions; the family tree (relatives, who their parents and partners are, birth years, birthplaces, life facts, stories and heritage notes) and each person's profile; who each person lets see each part of their profile; the meal plan and recipes; homework, pickups and drop-offs; pets and their care; family goals. These are saved in your browser's storage on your phone. We cannot see them.

Photos, profile photos and pictures of documents (ID cards, insurance cards, school forms and the like) are kept in a separate store on your phone. They are never put in the backup and never sent anywhere. Each one shows only to the people its "Who sees it" setting allows on that phone.

The parent PIN is a kid lock for a shared phone or tablet, kept on that phone. It is not a password and does not protect your data from someone who has your unlocked phone.

If other Hub apps are on the same phone, they can show a short read-only summary from Family Hub (and Family Hub from them); that happens on the phone and nothing is sent to us.

Deleting: ⚙ Settings → Your data → Erase everything deletes your Family Hub information on the phone (photos and document pictures included), signs the phone out of Google, tells Google to drop that sign-in, and forgets saved purchase emails and the AI passphrase for every Hub app on the phone. Removing one person: Family → Edit → Remove.

Children

Family Hub is used by parents and other adults to organize their family. Children do not sign up and do not have accounts, emails or passwords in Family Hub: a parent adds them as a profile (a first name, a face, a color and, if the parent chooses, a birthday). Family Hub does not collect anyone's location and does not keep medical records. A child can use Family Hub on a parent's phone or tablet in a child view that hides "adults only" items; going back to a parent's view asks for the parent PIN if one is set. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 13.

Sharing between phones

Sharing is something the family's owner turns on (Share → Turn on sharing). Until then nothing is sent. Once it is on:

  • Where it goes: a family space in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, United States), in a project run by Marvel Corp. Only the phones your family invited can open it: each phone signs in with a random, anonymous id (no name, email or password). Kids never sign in; they use a parent's phone or the family tablet.
  • What is sent: the family's name, members' first names, faces, colors and roles; the calendar, chores and check-offs, lists, the to-do board, meals and recipes, homework and pickups, pets, goals, the family tree, messages, reactions and announcements; and photos, at a smaller size. Each person's profile parts travel only as that person allows.
  • What is never sent: anything marked "Only me", Documents, the parent PIN, who is using the phone, Grandparent Mode, and profile pictures.
  • Who sees what: "Parents only" items, adults-only events and to-dos, the Parents conversation, allowance payments and the emergency card are kept in a part of the family space that a guest's phone (a sitter) cannot open. The emergency card reaches guests only if you allow it.
  • Invites: a 10-character code made for one person, good once, for 2 days.
  • Leaving: the owner can take any phone off the family, and anyone can stop sharing on their own phone. Their copy stays on their phone. The owner can link a Google account only to get the family back on a new phone; Google then knows only that you use Family Hub.
  • Firebase is run by Google (Firebase privacy). We don't look at your family's information, sell it or use it for advertising. Firebase Analytics is not used.

Google user data

Family Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features you turned on, in the app, on your phone. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and no person at Marvel Corp reads it. To disconnect, use ⚙ Settings in Family Hub or remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The backup copy can be deleted from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

Selling data

We do not sell or rent personal information. A family that shares between phones keeps its shared copy in Firebase for its own phones; we don't look at it.

Changes

If what Family Hub sends anywhere changes, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
